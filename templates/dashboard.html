ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

About TRACKBACK

Understand the platform, its purpose and how it helps investigate information.

What is TRACKBACK?

TRACKBACK is an AI-powered investigation platform designed to analyse suspicious claims and information. It examines available evidence and helps users understand whether a claim is supported, contradicted or needs further verification.


Our Main Purpose

To help users investigate information instead of blindly believing or sharing it.


What Makes TRACKBACK Different?

TRACKBACK focuses on investigation rather than simply giving a True or False answer. It analyses claims, examines evidence and highlights uncertainty.

SUPPORT CENTER

Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about using TRACKBACK.

What is TRACKBACK?

TRACKBACK is an AI-powered platform that investigates claims and analyses available evidence.

How do I start an investigation?

Open New Investigation, enter your claim and click Investigate Claim.

What does Supported mean?

The available evidence supports the claim.

What does Contradicted mean?

The available evidence conflicts with the claim.

What does Insufficient Evidence mean?

The available information is not enough to reach a reliable conclusion.

What does Needs Further Verification mean?

More investigation and evidence are required.

Can I investigate using voice?

Yes. Open New Investigation, select EN for English or ಕನ್ನಡ for Kannada, and click the microphone. Your speech will be converted into text in the claim box. You can review the text and click Investigate Claim.
